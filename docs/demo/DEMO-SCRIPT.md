# Beyond the Cards — Demo Script

Duration: 3 minutes 51 seconds. Resolution: 1920 × 1080. English narration and captions. Actual product screenshots from the public GitHub Pages demo. Voice: system-generated English narration.

## 01. Beyond the Cards

Beyond the Cards is an interactive tarot learning and storytelling experience. From the art of the cards to the story of you. Instead of simply receiving an answer, you explore visual symbols, connect them to your own experience, and shape a narrative that belongs to you.

## 02. The problem

For beginners, tarot can feel like a long list of meanings to memorize. The harder task is connecting imagery, card positions, different perspectives, and a real personal question. Our experience makes those connections visible, turning learning into a process of observation and creative expression.

## 03. Draw a beginning

Start by drawing a card in the same visual space. Here, the draw reveals Death. We treat the image as a prompt for change and transition, not a prediction of harm. The card stays visible as we explore its scene and consider what its imagery brings to mind.

## 04. See before interpreting

Selected cards also offer interactive symbol hotspots. In the library, we can open the Fool and explore the small dog, the white rose, or the cliff. Each point connects a visible detail with a teaching perspective. Other cards provide scene exploration without inventing precise symbol hotspots.

## 05. Compare perspectives

Back with our drawn card, the reversed option adds another perspective on the same image. We do not redraw or lose the original card. Here, an ending can also suggest reluctance to move on. These are possibilities to reflect on, and the user chooses what fits.

## 06. Bring in your voice

Now we record what we notice and what it reminds us of. A rider moving forward becomes an association with leaving a familiar role. These are user-written contributions, kept distinct from the teaching text. Personal meaning grows from the relationship between the picture and the person looking at it.

## 07. Add your context

Next, we add a situation: I want to leave a familiar job and start a creative project, but I am afraid to let go. We select uncertain as the feeling. Connecting this context produces a reflection draft that brings together the card, the chosen perspective, and our own words.

## 08. Choose a creative form

The reflection opens into a creative workspace. We can choose a personal story, a storyboard, a journal, a poem, a character story, or a Tarot Story Card. The current templates provide a starting structure, so the user can move from interpreting an image to expressing an idea.

## 09. Make it yours

We edit the title to Making Space for a New Chapter and rewrite the draft in our own voice. This passage is a user edit, not model-generated text. We save the work, and the studio also offers text copying and SVG story-card export. The result is a piece of personal expression.

## 10. Learn through relationships

The same learning approach also supports three cards: what I bring, what I am in, and what I can explore. Together, the positions offer a narrative structure. The searchable library contains all seventy-eight cards, so learners can return to specific imagery and continue building their visual vocabulary.

## 11. The AI pathway

This version uses local rules and narrative templates. A live AI model is not connected yet, and the interface states that clearly. The planned architecture combines tarot knowledge retrieval, visual evidence, user context, and structured model suggestions. The user will remain able to choose, question, and edit every interpretation.
